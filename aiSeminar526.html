Lesson Objectives

  • Understand the basic components of healthy nutrition.
  • Analyze healthy meal planning strategies.
  • Evaluate food choices using nutritional guidelines.
  • Create balanced meals using the MyPlate model.

Nutrition Planning Activities

Activity 1: Food Label Investigation

Students compare nutrition labels from packaged foods and identify sugar, sodium, and fiber content.

Activity 2: Healthy Grocery Budget

Students plan healthy meals while staying within a weekly grocery budget.

Activity 3: MyPlate Meal Design

Students design a balanced plate including fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, and dairy.

Activity 4: Nutrition Myth Debate

Students work in groups to discuss and challenge common nutrition myths.

Interactive Meal Planning Activity

Type a healthy breakfast idea and receive instructor feedback.

Include at least one protein, fruit, or whole grain.
